🏆 How to play & power-ups

You race as the CHICKEN 🐔 against REX 🐕, WOOLY 🐑, WHISKERS 🐈 and HOPPER 🐸 over 3 laps. Grab a colored cube for a power; each white cube upgrades it (max 3). Using a power spends everything!

Drive: W/▲ gas (release to coast to a stop) · S/▼ brake & reverse · A/D steer · Z look back · Space use power. Rivals shoot back!

🧱 Obstacle Course (toggle on the banner): before every race you and your rivals each place an obstacle on the road — walls, oil, an angry farmer or a champion kart.

Pick your racer, then tap a world to start · click the badge to toggle the Obstacle Course.
